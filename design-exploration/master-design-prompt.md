# EH / SPATIAL BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
## Master Design Prompt · Expanded Multi-Branch Operating System

**Version:** 2.0 · **Brief updated:** 17 November 2026  
**Product line:** Healthcare Operations × Financial Intelligence × Spatial Experience  
**Design status:** Concept brief only. This document does not describe implemented or integrated accounting functionality.

---

## 1. Product definition

Design **EH / SPATIAL BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE** as a **Multi-Branch Healthcare Business Operating System** for a growing physical-therapy group. It brings healthcare operations and financial/business oversight into one coherent product while keeping them as distinct, connected workspaces:

- **Healthcare Operations** — appointments, services, branch activity and operational indicators.
- **Financial & Business Operations** — invoice/collection oversight and a dedicated ERP workspace for financial control.
- **Spatial Experience** — an abstract, useful way to understand the relationship between the group and its branches. It is not a building model or clinic floor plan.

The home dashboard is a **Spatial Executive Command Center**: a group-level decision and navigation surface, not a crowded page of every ERP widget. It should help a group leader understand the consolidated picture, compare the three branches, see the supported operational context and enter a separate workspace for deeper financial work.

The underlying prototype is mock-driven. A visual concept must not imply a live accounting integration, verified financial ledger, deployed ERP capability or real patient record.

## 2. Reusable master prompt

Use the following as the shared prompt for each of the three concepts. Generate each concept as a **separate, standalone screen**; never combine them into a collage or comparison grid.

> Create a polished, high-fidelity desktop dashboard concept for **EH / SPATIAL BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE**, a bilingual, Arabic-first multi-branch healthcare business operating system for a physical-therapy group. The product connects Healthcare Operations with Financial & Business Operations through clear scope, useful spatial relationships and a dedicated ERP workspace.
>
> This is the **Spatial Executive Command Center**, not a patient-facing app and not a clinic booking screen. Lead with consolidated group intelligence and an abstract network of the three named prototype branches: **الغدير / Al Ghadeer (Branch 01)**, **الدار البيضاء / Al Dar Al Baida (Branch 02)** and **القويعية / Al Quwayiyah (Branch 03)**. Make it immediately clear which values belong to the group and which belong to an individual branch. A branch-selection interaction may focus its context and update the connected views; show a believable selected state without inventing data.
>
> Preserve a shared shell across concepts: an organization/branch scope, period and service filters, existing role/access context, navigation, language and theme controls, a visible demo-data indicator, and clear entry points into Healthcare Operations and a **separate Financial & Business Operations / ERP workspace**. The ERP must be a destination for detailed work, not a wall of compressed accounting widgets on the home dashboard.
>
> On the home dashboard, combine a restrained group summary, the three-branch comparison, distinct **invoiced/revenue** and **collected** values, the source-backed historical appointments/completions trend, and a compact daily-operations context. Use only the verified illustrative values and exact distinctions in this brief. Label them clearly as demo data. Do not blend invoices with collections, describe either as profit or cash-on-hand, or fabricate costs, profit, cash flow, payables, assets, stock balances, tax submissions or accounting entries.
>
> The branch network must be an abstract data relationship, not a map, building, floor plan, room layout or fabricated geography. Spatial form must explain group-to-branch scope or provide useful navigation; avoid decorative depth without meaning. Connect branch-level operational and financial context to the group summary without implying automatic clinical-to-ledger posting or a backend integration that has not been verified.
>
> The ERP workspace may be represented as a separate navigable area with modules for sales/accounts receivable, purchasing/accounts payable, general ledger, cash and bank, inventory, fixed assets, tax/e-invoicing and reports. Distinguish existing mock-driven prototype surfaces from unimplemented or unverified capabilities. Mark conceptual modules with a clear **تصوّري / مخطط / غير متاح في النموذج** or equivalent status; do not depict them as live or completed. Do not invent values for those modules.
>
> Make the screen Arabic-first with correct RTL reading order and natural Arabic labels; provide a deliberate English LTR counterpart, not a mechanical mirror. Keep mixed-script values readable, use tabular numerals for figures, maintain high contrast, keyboard-visible focus and touch-sized controls, and support narrow screens and reduced motion. Use calm, executive typography and generous hierarchy. No patient names, initials, IDs, faces, diagnoses, medical histories or identifiable records. No fake real-time indicators, fabricated alerts or misleading live-status dots.
>
> Create a credible product interface, not a marketing poster. Use only generic design inspiration from the supplied ERP/finance references; do not copy their logos, brand names, exact layouts or data. Prefer accurate labels and a few well-composed modules over dense illegible microcopy.

## 3. Shared system hierarchy and screen content

All three directions must portray the **same system, shared data, module scope and status language**. Only their visual composition and spatial metaphor should differ.

### 01 · Group Intelligence — مركز قيادة المجموعة

The group view is the default scope. It summarizes the supported branch comparison and shows which period and service filter are active. Use comparable measures only. Keep invoices/revenue separate from collected payments; show clinical activity as its own related stream rather than a substitute for financial performance.

### 02 · Spatial Branch Network — الشبكة المكانية للفروع

Show the three branches as clearly named, selectable data nodes. A branch node may show current source-backed invoiced and collected values and supported operational context. The group hub represents aggregation, not a fourth branch. Selecting a node should communicate a focused branch scope; returning to group scope should restore all three.

Use no real map, pin coordinates, clinic outline, room layout or invented distances. The branch names and their relationships are organizational data, not geographic claims.

### 03 · Financial Control — مركز الرقابة المالية

Financial Control is a **separate ERP workspace** with its own navigation and detail views. On the executive dashboard, represent it with a compact workspace entry point and, only where appropriate, a supported high-level invoice/collection summary. Do not cram every accounting module into the dashboard.

### 04 · Business Intelligence — مركز التحليل والتقارير

Business Intelligence is a separate analysis/reporting workspace. The dashboard may show the existing historical appointment trend and links to reports. Do not label a report as available or calculate it from missing source data. Show its readiness or dependency when it is only a future requirement.

## 4. Current prototype versus target accounting scope

The following distinctions are mandatory in every concept. **A module in the design map is not evidence that the feature exists.** Existing prototype areas identified in the brief are mock-driven and are not proof of a real accounting integration.

| Unit | Organizational scope | Prototype/design status |
|---|---|---|
| Sales / Accounts Receivable | Each branch | Invoices and collections exist as mock-driven prototype surfaces; do not imply verified ledger linkage or real payment processing. |
| Purchases / Accounts Payable | Each branch | Purchases are mock-driven in the prototype description; no verified AP ledger or supplier settlement integration. |
| General Ledger | Each branch | Required target capability; not implemented or verified. Do not show posted or automated journal entries as real. |
| Cash & Bank | Each branch | Required target capability; not implemented or verified. No bank balances or cash-flow totals may be invented. |
| Inventory | Each branch | An inventory prototype surface is described; it is mock-driven, not proof of a verified stock ledger or valuation. |
| Fixed Assets | Each branch | Required target capability; not implemented or verified. |
| ZATCA & e-invoicing | Company level | Required target capability; no live ZATCA integration or submission status is established. |
| Financial Statements | Consolidated across branches | Required target capability; no verified consolidation, elimination or accounting basis is established. |
| Accounting Reports | Branch and group | Report views are mock-driven; do not present them as validated accounting statements. |
| Sales Reports | Branch and group | Mock reporting may be shown only as illustrative. Validate sales, service and specialist attribution before presenting it as business truth. |

Treat **General Ledger, automatic journal entries, trial balance, cash/bank, fixed assets, ZATCA integration, financial statements and consolidation** as unimplemented/unverified requirements unless separately proven. Use a subdued planned/not-available state rather than a green active badge, success indicator or fabricated transaction list.

## 5. Business Intelligence report readiness

The following report ideas belong in the design brief, but each has a data prerequisite. Do not create realistic-looking values where the necessary source data is absent.

| Analysis | What it should explain | Required evidence before showing real figures |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue vs Collections | Difference between invoiced sales/revenue and amounts collected | Verified invoice and receipt linkage; keep the two series distinct. |
| Branch Profitability | Comparable financial performance by branch | Verified expenses/costs and allocation rules. Revenue less collections is not profit. |
| Cash Flow | Cash movement over time | Verified cash/bank transactions and opening/closing balances. Collections alone are not cash flow. |
| Receivables Aging | Unsettled customer balances by age | Invoice due dates, settlements and a validated aging rule. |
| Payables Aging | Unsettled supplier balances by age | Supplier invoices, due dates, payments and a validated aging rule. |
| Cost Centers | Distribution of costs | Confirmed cost-center assignments and allocation data. |
| Specialist Performance | Attributed sales/activity by specialist | Verified specialist attribution and billing/transaction data. Appointment counts alone are not sales. |
| Service Performance | Sales/activity by service | Verified service-line billing and a stable service taxonomy. |
| Payment Methods | Collections by payment method | Actual receipt/payment-method records. |
| Consolidated Reports | Comparable branch and group results | Verified accounting structure, consolidation logic and any required eliminations. |

The requested **Saudi / non-Saudi** distinction in specialist/service sales reports is a future data-definition requirement. State how each category is defined and sourced before using it. Never infer nationality from a patient name, appearance or other proxy, and do not include patient-level identity in a dashboard concept.

## 7. Three visual directions — same system, different composition

### 01 — Spatial Command Center · مركز القيادة المكاني

**Selected direction to prioritize when the concepts are regenerated.** Make the three branch nodes the visual anchor and connect them to one group-intelligence hub. Give every node its own readable branch label and a restrained financial/operational summary. The group hub can show the supported aggregate snapshot. A selected node should make the scope change legible and preserve the distinction between branch and group values.

The screen must communicate a multi-branch healthcare **business** at first glance: place invoice/collection comparison beside operational activity and the historical trend, and provide clear entry points to Financial Control and Business Intelligence workspaces. It must not read as a clinic appointment calendar with decorative branches.

**Material/palette:** calm ink and charcoal, mineral teal, quiet neutral surfaces and precise connective lines. Use spatial depth only to clarify hierarchy or focus. No neon, cyberpunk HUD, glowing live-status dots or decorative fake telemetry. Do not render literal architecture or a floor plan.

**Interaction proposal:** selecting a branch focuses its branch context and connected comparisons; selecting group scope restores the consolidated view. These are design interactions, not claims about implementation.

### 02 — Architectural Digital Twin · التوأم الرقمي المعماري

Use layered, architectural **data planes** rather than buildings: each branch is a distinct organizational layer and the consolidated group is an explicitly labeled synthesis plane. Keep the same three branch names, same metrics, same filters, same finance/operations pairing and same ERP/report destinations as Concept 01. Make branch-versus-group scope exceptionally easy to compare.

**Material/palette:** warm mineral/bone, stone, graphite and restrained teal; editorial spacing and hairline separators. No literal clinic, rooms, medical equipment or floor plan. Avoid implying that the app has a digital twin of the physical sites.

### 03 — Immersive Clinical & Financial Operations · العمليات السريرية والمالية

Use the daily operational sequence as the entry into a broader business view. Place the selected day's anonymized schedule and branch/service context alongside a clear invoiced-versus-collected summary and the same branch comparison/history. Show how the two workspaces relate without claiming that an appointment automatically creates an invoice or posts a journal entry.

**Material/palette:** graphite and warm neutral surfaces, mineral teal and one restrained warm accent. Use a disciplined timeline or flow to clarify activity, not dramatic overlays or motion. Keep Financial Control and Business Intelligence as separate destinations, not a dense stack of unverified accounting widgets.

## 8. Localization, accessibility and visual guardrails

- Arabic is the primary design direction. Use natural Arabic copy, correct RTL hierarchy, right-aligned labels and intentional Arabic number treatment. English must have a considered LTR layout; do not mechanically mirror icons, chart axes or numbers.
- Keep SAR values, percentages and time in readable isolated directionality (`dir="ltr"`/equivalent where appropriate). Keep Arabic and Latin text from colliding.
- Preserve the prototype's existing role/access model, navigation, branch context, theme and language controls in any future implementation; do not invent role permissions or pretend a planned module is accessible.
- Design keyboard selection and visible focus, touch targets, responsive stacking/scrolling and reduced-motion alternatives. Do not rely on color alone to convey a selected branch, status or financial distinction.
- Avoid generic SaaS card grids, tiny unreadable microcopy, medical stock photography, patient avatars, identifiable records, fictional financial balances, invented branch locations, false real-time signals and misleading success/connection states.
- External reference links supplied with the brief are visual inspiration only. Do not copy third-party logos, labels, data or exact compositions.

## 9. Deliverable and acceptance checklist

Produce **three separate standalone concept screens**, one per direction, with identical product scope, core data, branch names, filters, demo labeling and module-readiness truth. Do not put multiple concepts in one image. The images are explorations, not working software.

A concept is ready for review only if:

1. It reads as a multi-branch healthcare business operating system, not only a clinic schedule.
2. The group view and all three named branches are explicit; branch and group scopes are not conflated.
3. Invoiced/revenue and collected amounts are visibly separate; no profit or cash-flow claim is inferred.
4. Operational context, historical performance and the separate ERP/report workspaces are present without cramming every module onto the home page.
5. Existing mock-driven prototype surfaces and future/unverified accounting requirements are not presented as equivalent or live.
6. All shared mock figures match this brief and are labeled illustrative/demo; no patient-identifying content appears.
7. Concept 01 makes the branch network meaningful and gives equal visual credibility to business/financial and healthcare operations.
8. Arabic RTL and English LTR, accessibility, responsive behavior and reduced motion are considered.
