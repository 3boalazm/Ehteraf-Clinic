# EH / SPATIAL BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
## Expanded Design Exploration

The product direction is now **Multi-Branch Healthcare Business Operating System** — connecting Healthcare Operations, Financial Intelligence and Spatial Experience while keeping detailed ERP work in a separate workspace.

### Master prompt

The updated, reusable brief for all three concepts is in [`master-design-prompt.md`](./master-design-prompt.md). It defines:

- One shared multi-branch system and verified illustrative data contract for all three visual directions.
- **Concept 01 — Spatial Command Center** as the group-level branch network, with financial and operational context together.
- Financial Control and Business Intelligence as distinct ERP/report workspaces, not a dense collection of dashboard widgets.
- The organizational scope and readiness status of accounting modules, including capabilities that remain unimplemented or unverified.
- Arabic-first RTL, intentional English LTR, accessibility, privacy and data-integrity requirements.

### Status and interpretation

- The **master prompt is a design artifact**; it does not implement, integrate or certify accounting/ERP functionality. The separate, later-authorized application change implements only the Concept 01 dashboard surface.
- Existing invoice, collection, purchase, inventory, report, appointment and patient areas described for the prototype are mock-driven; they are not proof of live accounting or external integrations.
- General Ledger, automated journals, trial balance, Cash & Bank, Fixed Assets, ZATCA, financial statements and consolidation must not be portrayed as implemented unless independently verified.
- The numeric examples in the master prompt were checked against the repository's mock sources and are explicitly illustrative. Do not copy unverified values from artwork or add patient-identifying content.
- The three PNGs currently in this folder are the **earlier first-pass visual explorations**. They predate the expanded business-system brief and have not been regenerated. They should not be read as showing the expanded scope or as evidence that ERP functions exist.
- The original prompt-only update did not change application code or images. A later authorized Concept 01 dashboard implementation is being handled separately and uses existing mock data only; it does not add or claim accounting/ERP functionality.
